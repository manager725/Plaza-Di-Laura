What we found

Where today's site loses clients

The current site looks clean. Underneath, three things send people away before they book. We checked every page on 24 November 2026.

01

Boka leads to 242 other salons

Every booking and review button points to your old Bokadirekt profile. It now redirects to Bokadirekt's list of skin clinics in Göteborg, with your competitors first.

02

Course advice opens another salon

The course advice button on Om Laura lands on a 90-minute facial at Lily's Beauty Salon, at the moment someone is ready to talk about a 55 000 kr course.

03

No way to call, email or visit

No phone number, email, opening hours or map anywhere on the site, and some numbers disagree: customers are "1500+" on one page and "över 15 000" on another.

The design

Calm, precise, unmistakably yours

Your brand, kept

The thin, widely spaced PLAZA DI LAURA wordmark stays exactly as it is. Your studio portraits in white and your close-up results carry the pages.

One colour, taken from your work

Warm porcelain and charcoal, plus a single clay rose taken from your lip results. It marks buttons and accents, never more.

Couture typography

Headings in Cormorant Garamond, the refined serif of fashion and beauty houses, with one word in rose italic. Text in Jost, whose thin geometric letters echo your logo.

Every section composed

Each part of the home page has its own layout, like the spreads of a magazine. Motion is gentle, and visitors who prefer less movement see a still page.

Plaza Di Laura · A new bylaura.se04 · Booking
Booking

From interest to booked in four steps

Booking happens on your own site, not on a marketplace next to your competitors. A summary with the treatment photo follows the client through every step.

1

Choose. Treatments as photo cards in three groups, or a free consultation.

2

Pick days. Up to three days and a time of day, or "I'm flexible".

3

Health check. One yes or no per question from your treatment pages. Any yes kindly offers a free consultation instead.

4

Confirmed. A reference number and preparation tips for that exact treatment; for lips, exfoliate three days before.

The same four steps on a phone

A bar at the bottom of the screen always shows what has been chosen and the next step. Every control is large enough for a thumb, and the visitor can go back to any step without losing what they filled in.
