Technical overview

How the new bylaura.se is built

A fast, static website with nothing to install and very little to attack. This page explains the build; the next two cover speed, privacy, testing and what it takes to go live.

From your content to a visitor's screen

Pages are assembled ahead of time, so a phone receives a finished page instead of waiting for a website builder. There is no database or admin login on the public site.

What a visitor gets

  • Clinic: nine treatment pages with process, preparation, aftercare and timing.
  • Academy: six course pages with length, price where known, and course advice booking.
  • Tools: booking in four steps, Brynguiden, a before and after slider, quick-answer chat.

What stays with us

  • Source and design files are kept in our private repository and are not part of the published site.
  • Published code is compressed, which also makes it lighter to load.
  • Every version is kept, so any change can be undone in minutes.

Plaza Di Laura · Technical overview02 · Speed, access, privacy
Quality in use

Fast, open to everyone, private by default

Speed

  • Right-sized images. Every photo is saved in the modern WebP format at the sizes it is shown, and the browser picks the smallest sharp one. Today's home photo is a 2.5 MB PNG.
  • Visible first. The main photo loads with priority; photos further down load as the visitor scrolls.
  • Light code. No plugins or frameworks. One page, one stylesheet and one script come to about 70 KB compressed.
  • Own fonts. About 100 KB, served from the site itself.
  • Smooth motion. Animations only move and fade, which the phone's graphics chip handles.

Every device

  • Phones first. Designed for the phone, expanded for tablets and computers, with its own layout for landscape phones.
  • Easy to tap. Every control at least 44 px on touch screens.
  • Easy to read. Text contrast meets WCAG 2.1 AA in both themes.
  • Keyboard and screen readers. Menus, booking, Brynguiden and the gallery work without a mouse; photos are described.
  • Less motion on request. Visitors who turn off animations see a still page.

Privacy

  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising cookies, so no cookie banner is needed.
  • No outside calls. Fonts and photos come from the site itself. The Google map loads only when a visitor clicks to show it.
  • On the visitor's device only: their language, theme, and whether they have seen the intro.
  • Preview forms check the details but send nothing yet.
The live Kundkort handles health data. Client records, health declarations and treatment photos are sensitive personal data under GDPR. The live version needs storage in the EU with encryption, personal BankID logins, a log of who opened which record, signed consent, and a data processing agreement between you and us. We plan this with you before any real client data is stored.

Plaza Di Laura · Technical overview03 · Hosting and launch
Hosting and updates

We look after it, you focus on clients

  • Where it runs. On Vercel, a professional hosting network that serves the site from servers close to each visitor, over an encrypted connection.
  • Private until launch. You get a private link to share with whoever you like. Search engines are told not to list it, so it does not compete with your current site.
  • Changes. Send us feedback, texts or photos; the updated site is online within minutes.
  • Your current site stays exactly as it is until you decide to switch.
