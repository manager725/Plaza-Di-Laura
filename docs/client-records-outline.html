Client records and booking for Plaza Di Laura

You showed us a client record with history, treatments, bookings, consent, payments, medical record, documentation photos and contact. We have added a working demo of exactly that to your prototype, under Kundkort (link in the footer of the demo site), and connected it to the new booking page.

How it works day to day

  1. The client books on your website, or books a free consultation.
  2. They answer your health questions. If one applies, the system suggests a consultation instead.
  3. They sign the consent form on their phone with BankID before the visit.
  4. A client card is created. You add method, pigment, needle and notes after the treatment.
  5. You take before, right-after and healed photos straight into the card.
  6. The system reminds you and the client about the touch-up after 4–6 weeks and the refill within 12 months.

Keeping client data safe

Health answers and face photos are sensitive personal data under GDPR. Whichever way we build, the data is stored encrypted within the EU, each staff member has their own login, access is logged, clients give consent, and records can be exported or deleted on request.
